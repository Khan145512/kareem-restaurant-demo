Our Story

The Lumière Legacy

Est. 1998

A Culinary Journey of Excellence

For over two decades, Lumière has stood as a beacon of gastronomic innovation in the heart of the city. Founded by the visionary Chef Antoine Delacroix, our restaurant combines classical French techniques with contemporary global influences.

Every plate that leaves our kitchen is a carefully composed symphony of flavors, textures, and aromas — designed to evoke emotion and create lasting memories. We source only the finest seasonal ingredients from sustainable farms and artisan producers.

Meet The Master

Executive Chef

Antoine Delacroix

Executive Chef & Founder

With a career spanning over three decades across Paris, Tokyo, and New York, Chef Antoine brings a world of experience to every dish. His philosophy centers on respecting ingredients while pushing the boundaries of culinary art. Trained at Le Cordon Bleu and mentored by legendary chefs, he has crafted a menu that speaks to both tradition and innovation.

"Cooking is not just about feeding the body — it's about nourishing the soul and creating moments that linger long after the last bite."

— A. Delacroix
